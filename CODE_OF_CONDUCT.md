# Community code of conduct

Treat participants with respect. Discuss evidence and ideas, welcome questions,
and give specific, constructive feedback. Harassment, threats, discriminatory
abuse, unwanted sexual attention, and disclosure of someone else's private
information are not acceptable.

These expectations apply to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other spaces maintained for Jev-Mem. They also apply when representing the
project in public.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict
participation, or ban repeated or severe violations. Responses should be
proportionate and consider the affected participants' privacy. Do not retaliate
against someone reporting a concern.

To report a concern, contact a repository maintainer privately through a contact
method they have published. If none is available, request a private channel
without posting personal information or details of the incident publicly.
